Add a generic Kanban Board workspace family (`@lapismd/design-core/workspace/kanban`) ported from the legacy Fizzy task board: collapsible columns with options menus and colour swatches, `@dnd-kit/svelte` pointer and keyboard drag & drop with a drag overlay, the sticky pinned-card corner, golden tickets and stamps, the full-screen card detail dialog with a consumer `body` snippet, and the full-screen column view. Grouping, persistence and card body content remain consumer responsibilities.
