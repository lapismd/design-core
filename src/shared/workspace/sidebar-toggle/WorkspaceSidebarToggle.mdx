# WorkspaceSidebarToggle

Shows or hides a left or right workspace sidebar. The indicator stays on the
matching side of the source-shaped icon.

## Usage

<Source
  language="tsx"
  code={
    '<WorkspaceSidebarToggle side="right" label="Toggle right sidebar" onSelect={toggleRight} />'
  }
/>
