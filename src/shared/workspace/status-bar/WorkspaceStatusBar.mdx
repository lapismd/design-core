# Workspace status bar

The status bar renders controller contributions in a dedicated full-width footer
with left, bounded center and right regions. The center retains its width while
idle; long activity labels truncate without moving side contributions. Lower
priority side entries remain available in overflow popovers. App Shell reserves
space beneath its desktop content, including the ribbon and both sidebars.

Hosts may set `--ui-workspace-safe-area-inline-start`,
`--ui-workspace-safe-area-inline-end` and `--ui-workspace-safe-area-block-end`
on a Workspace ancestor. Defaults are `0px`. The footer adds the larger inline
inset to both edges so its centre stays aligned with the app; the block-end
inset increases the footer track while retaining its 1.5rem content height.
The shell background and separator still span the full window width.
