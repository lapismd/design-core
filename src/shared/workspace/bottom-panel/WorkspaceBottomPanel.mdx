# WorkspaceBottomPanel

`WorkspaceBottomPanel` renders the workspace's bottom dock beneath the main
editor body. Its top-level views use a horizontal tab strip. A grouped tab
transposes the sidebar-group mechanics: panels resize left-to-right and a
collapsed panel remains available as a vertical title rail.

The layout and controller own open state, height, tab order, grouping,
collapsed panels, and expanded sizes. Consumers own terminal processes and
other panel runtimes through registered workspace views.

On mobile, panel views belong to the open-tabs page and render as full-screen
views rather than as a dock or bottom sheet.
