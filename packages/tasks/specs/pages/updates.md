# Updates page spec

Updates is an activity/inbox surface with filters for **All**, **Tasks**,
**Messages**, and **Lists**. The observed reference includes a deliberately
calm empty state. Preserve that hierarchy: filter controls at the top; centered
empty feedback with a short explanation and no fake activity below.

Future activity rows must have a clear actor, object, action, timestamp, and a
safe link to the affected task/list. Do not require colour or avatar imagery to
understand an update.
